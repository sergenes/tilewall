### Better

- **Equal Tiles ends per display.** With a group on each of two displays, focusing a tile on one screen grows that tile and leaves the other screen's tiles equal. In 0.3.0 it ended on both screens at once.
- The help window describes shift drag, Equal Tiles, Always Tile and the update check.
- The repository has a changelog, generated from the release notes.

### Checked

- Two groups on two displays were tested end to end for this release: sending tiles across, each group keeping its own enlarged tile, focus cycling and reordering inside one group, shift drag inside a group and onto the other display's group, dragging and resizing one group, switching tiling off and on for one display, dropping a group on the other display, and a restart.
