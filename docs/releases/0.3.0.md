### New

- **Drag to reorder.** Hold shift while dragging a tile by its title bar. Drop it on another tile and the two trade places. Dropped on a tile of another display, it joins that group in front of that tile. Dropped anywhere else, it goes back. A drag without shift still moves the whole group.
- **Equal Tiles.** Makes every tile the same size, so nothing is enlarged. The tiles stay equal until you focus one, which then grows again. In the menu, on ctrl+alt+e, and as `tilewall even [on|off]`.
